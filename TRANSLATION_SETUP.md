## 🚀 Quick Setup (2 minutes)

### Option 1: Google Gemini (Recommended - Free & Most Advanced)

Google Gemini Flash 2.0 provides **state-of-the-art translation** with cultural context and medical accuracy.

1. **Get your FREE API key**:
   - Visit: https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
   - Click "Create API Key"
   - Copy your key

2. **Add to your app**:
   Open `lib/constants/app_constants.dart` line 27 and replace:
   ```dart
   static const String geminiApiKey = 'YOUR_GEMINI_API_KEY';
   ```
   with your actual key.

3. **Done!** 🎉 Your app now supports instant AI translation!
